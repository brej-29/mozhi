# Voice observatory

The requested command deck becomes an observatory for language: a dimensional mint signal sphere, orbit geometry, deep ink ground, ivory typography and a separate decision journal. Existing Bricolage Grotesque gives headings a human voice; Instrument Sans keeps dense controls clear. Daylight uses pale mineral green with dark teal ink.

The sphere communicates real idle, listening, thinking, speaking and declined states. The background is quiet signal geometry, never invented telemetry. Cards reveal readable content immediately, with word focus and reading progress driven by browser boundaries. Reduced motion is a still composition. Phone controls precede the journal.
