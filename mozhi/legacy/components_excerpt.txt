# Components Overview

This document describes the main components of the Jarvis Voice Agent codebase and how they fit together.

---

## Repository Layout

At a high level:

```text
/
  README.md
  .gitignore
  context/
    GOAL.md
    ARCHITECTURE.md
    COMPONENTS.md
    API_CONTRACT.md
    COSINE_GROUNDING.md
    DECISIONS.md
    CHANGELOG.md
  backend/
    app/
      core/
      schemas/
      services/
      routers/
    tests/
    requirements.txt (or pyproject.toml, depending on choice)
    .env.example
  mobile/
    pubspec.yaml
    lib/
      core/
      features/
        assistant/
        settings/
    android/...
    ios/...
```

- Keep **context** close to the code, for humans and AI tools.
